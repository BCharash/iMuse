Important architecture test: Voicute listens for the wake phrase until detection, then its inference and ring-buffer writes suspend while TensorFlow listens; the Voicute microphone track stays open. Joanna “Yes?” is preloaded; TensorFlow starts only after wake detection, then releases its own microphone capture after Go, Stop, or timeout. During the command window TensorFlow still uses a separate microphone capture; this is not yet true single-stream sharing. No production recordings are made.
